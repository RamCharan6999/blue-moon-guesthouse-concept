04 · Hosted by José & Ângela

A guesthouse
with people behind it.

José & Ângela describe Blue Moon as a personal guesthouse where they enjoy meeting travelers, sharing local information and helping guests settle into Lagos. They ask guests to share their arrival time in advance so they can provide a welcoming check-in.

Direct contact, local knowledge and a more personal check-in are part of the Blue Moon experience.
